FAQ

Good questions to ask before you hire

Can't find yours? Ask me directly — I reply within one business day.

Do you work inside my existing GoHighLevel account?

Yes. Most projects happen inside your own sub-account (or your agency account). I can also build reusable snapshots if you're an agency deploying the same setup to many clients.

Can you fix a messy GHL account someone else set up?

Often that's the best place to start. I audit your workflows, tags and pipelines, remove duplicates and loops, and rebuild only what needs rebuilding — documented, so your team understands it.

How much does a typical automation project cost?

Smaller fixes and single workflows start around a few hundred dollars. Full CRM builds with funnels, calendars and integrations are scoped after a free audit call, always as one fixed price.

I'm on HubSpot, Keap or ClickFunnels. Can you migrate me?

Yes — contacts, tags, custom fields, pipelines and email templates. I map everything first so nothing important is lost in the move.

Do you also build the website?

Yes. I've built 140+ sites on WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Kajabi and more. I can build it in GHL or keep your current site and connect its forms to your CRM.

What time zone are you in?

Pakistan (GMT+5). I overlap with UK and Europe mornings and US East Coast mornings, and keep updates async with Loom videos.
